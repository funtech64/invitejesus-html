Our Mission

Why this site exists

InviteJesus is not a directory of religions; it is an apologetic defense. Its single aim is to show how an evangelical reading of John 14:6 could be true — and why that claim deserves the careful attention of any honest seeker.

✦

Thoughtful

We engage the hardest questions on their own terms, treating the evidence with the same scrutiny we would ask of any extraordinary claim.

✦

Rigorous

History, philosophy, probability, and textual scholarship are brought to bear so that faith rests on reasoned foundations, not wishful thinking.

✦

Respectful

Every tradition is described fairly before it is examined. We persuade by the weight of the case, never by caricature of those who differ.
